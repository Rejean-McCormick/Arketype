# Testimony-specific boundaries

An attributed statement records **what someone said**, not whether the statement is true. Keep the declarant's words separate from interviewer questions, verbatim transcripts, translations, corrections, signed attestations and external corroboration. Preserve source media, versions and collection timestamps according to applicable policy.

Consent to speak, consent to recording and permission for public dissemination are different decisions. Legal status, chain of custody, access control and retention require qualified operational procedures, not just a portable knowledge template.
